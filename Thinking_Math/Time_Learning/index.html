Level 1: Minute hand math

Look at the clock.

Clock Math Guide

Minute hand Each number is 5 minutes.
Hour hand Short blue hand shows the hour.
Past Right half: count after the hour.
To Left half: count until the next hour.

Use math first: 1 = 5 minutes, 2 = 10 minutes, 3 = 15 minutes. On the left side, subtract from 60 to find how many minutes are left before the next hour.
