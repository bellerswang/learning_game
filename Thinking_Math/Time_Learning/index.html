How to read this clock

🔴 Long red hand Points to minutes. Each big number is 5 minutes.
🔵 Short blue hand Shows the hour. It moves between numbers as time passes.
Past From 12 to 6: minutes after the hour.
To From 6 to 12: minutes until the next hour.

Try a clue any time. A wrong answer is a chance to look again—there is no timer. The little button in the middle lets you watch a second hand tick; it does not change the answer.
